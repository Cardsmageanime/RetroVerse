# Nexus Relay — unified product identity and architecture

**Working product name:** **Nexus Relay** (owner-selected, October 26, 2026).
**Previously separate concepts:** Arcade Nexus and Retro Relay Online (RRO).
**Status:** architecture/branding decision; features not automatically implemented.
**Scope:** Phases 1–5 only. **Phase 6 remains reserved and off-limits.**

## One product, distinct modules
- **Arcade World:** originally Arcade Nexus's 2D side-scrolling social arcade; 3D world and avatars are future upgrades, not existing features.
- **Gaming History Museum:** accurately sourced console/game history, era galleries, development timelines and best-selling titles; multilingual content.
- **Library and Offline System:** ARM-compatible removable SD/USB boot foundation, RetroArch-based offline play, controllers, local saves and independent use without Wi-Fi/account. Protect internal Android/eMMC.
- **Online Relay:** identity, security, friends, public/private/creator/spectator lobbies, and compatible multiplayer netplay. No multiplayer cheats.
- **Personal Arcade Home:** friend-visible by default; private gaming sessions and customizable personal spaces.
- **Nexus Relay Store:** future marketplace for **independently authorized/licensed** arcade titles from publishers/partners. Rights verification, product permissions, payment security, refunds, taxes and regional compliance must be addressed before commerce is enabled. No rights assumed.
- **Original visual identity:** entirely new logos, interface, icons, music and media (owned or explicitly licensed). Reference research is not permission to copy.

## Naming and compatibility
- Use **Nexus Relay** for new user-facing documentation and UI design.
- Existing GitHub repository `Cardsmageanime/RetroVerse`, branch identifiers and tools should remain untouched pending a tested migration plan; the biweekly task points to this repository.
- Existing Retro Relay **PC application** history should be reviewed and reconciled before any deletion or full repository merger. A unified product can still use separate components/platform builds.
- Old branding identifiers are historical references, not a directive to reuse their assets.
- This is a **working name**, not legal name or trademark clearance.
